AI is not a shortcut. It is a force multiplier — and in 2024–2025, it became a genuine competitive advantage in security work.

By the end of 2024, LLM agents were solving 95%+ of beginner CTF challenges autonomously. In 2025, one AI system placed first at a live competition and took a $50,000 prize. An autonomous pen-testing AI reached #1 on HackerOne's US leaderboard.

This section covers what AI can actually do, which models to use, how to set them up, and how to build workflows and agents for security work.
