Contributing is not only code. Fixing a wrong sentence on a wiki page, sitting
with a newer member through a challenge, or pointing someone at the right
resource all count, and all of them make the club better.

The lowest-effort, highest-value thing you can do is fix what is already here.
If a page is wrong, out of date, or missing, that is an opening, not a
complaint — open a pull request.

---

## Two Ways In

- **Fix the wiki.** The [Authoring guide](/meta/authoring/) covers how pages
  are written and submitted. Find something broken and fix it.
- **Help a person.** Answer a question in [Discord](http://discord.gg/2Yun8WAUuy).
  Explaining something you just learned is the fastest way to be sure you
  actually learned it.
