A writeup is the step where a solve turns into knowledge you keep. You explain
what the challenge was, what you tried, what actually worked, and why — in
enough detail that the next person can follow it.

You do not need a polished essay. A rough, honest account of the path you took
beats a clean one that hides the dead ends, because the dead ends are the part
people learn from.

---

## Where They Go

Push writeups to the club's GitHub, or open a pull request against this wiki.
The [competition archive](/compete/archive/) links the repos and writeups past
teams have already published — read a few before you write your first to see
the shape.
