A cert does not make you good. It gets you past the HR filter so someone who
is good can talk to you. Get one early, then go build things — the cert is
the door, not the room.

## Which one, in what order

- **[Google Cybersecurity Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/programs/florida-atlantic-uni-google-learning-program-aacys)** — free through FAU's Coursera program. No prerequisites. Start here if you have never touched security.
- **[CompTIA Security+](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security)** — the standard entry-level cert. Vendor-neutral, widely recognized by HR filters. This is the one most students should get first.
- **[Offensive Security (OSCP)](https://www.offsec.com)** — a certification that tests actual hands-on exploitation, not multiple choice. Respected because it's hard.
- **[GIAC](https://www.giac.org)** — certifications tied to SANS courses, deep and specific per topic. Expensive; the material is excellent.
- **[(ISC)² CISSP](https://www.isc2.org)** — a management-and-breadth cert for more senior roles. Not your first cert as a student.

## Free study material

- **[Professor Messer](https://www.professormesser.com)** — free video courses for Security+ and Network+. Watch the full playlist before you pay for anything.
- **[Security+ Practice Quiz (SY0-701)](https://secquizmaster.pythonanywhere.com/sec)** — free practice questions for the current exam version. Take it after each Messer section, not before.
