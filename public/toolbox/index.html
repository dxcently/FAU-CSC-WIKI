Toolbox

This is where you come to find things. Tools, learning material, reference guides — the good stuff that actually helps when you sit down and try to do something real.

Here is the honest truth though: nobody is going to hand it to you, and that is fine. The resources here exist to point you in a direction, but whether you actually go anywhere is entirely up to you and what you do with your own time. The people who really get good at this are the ones who go home and keep going. Meetings are a starting point, not the whole picture.

Get comfortable reading documentation. Break things on purpose. When something does not make sense, lean into it — that confusion is usually where the best learning happens.

This section contains:

  • Links — curated external tools, learning platforms, and references we actually use and recommend.
  • Cheat Sheets — quick reference for the commands, ports, and triage steps you look up constantly.
  • Cybersecurity Intern Training Resource Guide — a difficulty-tiered roadmap of external resources, from Linux basics to advanced exploit development.
  • Choosing an AI Model — the criteria that actually matter when picking a model for security work.
  • AI Workflows & Agents — the agent loop and practical patterns for using AI in CTFs and hardening reviews.
  • Links Curated external tools, platforms, and references, grouped by what you are trying to do.
  • 🛡️ Cybersecurity Intern Training Resource Guide (2025) A difficulty-tiered roadmap of external resources for building Linux, Windows, cloud, web, and competition skills.
  • Cheat Sheets Quick reference for the commands, ports, and triage steps club members look up constantly.
  • Choosing an AI Model Selection criteria for picking an AI model for security work — not a product comparison.
  • AI Workflows & Agents The agent loop, common agent patterns, and vendor-neutral workflows for using AI in security work.
  • Automating Security Work How to wire a model into a repeatable pipeline for triage, summarizing, and first-pass review — and what never goes in the prompt.

Dig in, have fun with it, and do not be afraid to go down a rabbit hole or two. That is kind of the whole point.
