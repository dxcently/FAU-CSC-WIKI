Contributing to the Club

This is where learning turns into doing. Competing, writing writeups, helping newer members, building tools — this is where skills compound fast.

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. You just need to show up and try.

Ways to Contribute

Compete

Show up to competitions even if you do not know what you are doing. Watching how others solve problems is worth more than a week of tutorials.

Join the club’s team for CCDC, CyberPatriot, CTFs, and hackathons. See Past Competitions for what we have done.

Write Writeups

After a CTF or competition, write down what you solved and how. Even a rough explanation is useful. It forces you to understand what you actually did, and it helps the next person who hits the same problem.

Push writeups to the club’s GitHub or open a pull request on this wiki.

Contribute to the Wiki

Find a page that is missing or wrong. Fix it. Add it. Open a pull request.

See the Authoring guide for how to write pages and submit them.

Help Newer Members

If you know something someone else does not, explain it. Answering questions is one of the best ways to solidify your own knowledge.

Point people to resources. Sit with them through a challenge. You do not need to hand them the answer — just point them in the right direction.
